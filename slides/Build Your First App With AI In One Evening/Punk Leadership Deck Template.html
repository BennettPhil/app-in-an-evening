The loop

Four moves. On repeat.

Step 01
Describe
Say what you want in plain English. No jargon required.
Step 02
Run
Hit enter. Let the terminal do the boring part for you.
Step 03
Look
Read the output. Spot what's wrong. You'll know — trust it.
Step 04
Ship
Push it live. Break things. Learn the next thing. Repeat.
